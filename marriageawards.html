MARRIAGE INCENTIVE AWARD SCHEMES

MARRIAGE INCENTIVE AWARD (Disabled Welfare)

The links for Application Registration/Print/status/Edit Application for Marriage Incentive Award of Disabled Welfare are available beside

GOVERNMENT ORDERS :
MIA G.O.2.PDF (Guidelines)
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA :
  1. 1) One among the beneficiary, bride or bridegroom must belong to Disabled Category.
  2. 2) Registration should be done before one year from the marriage date.
DOCUMENTS & DETAILS REQUIRED :
  1. 1) Aadhar of both Bride and Bridegroom
  2. 2) Bank Account Details of Person With Disability
  3. 3) Marriage Registration Certificate
  4. 4) Permanent & Present Address details of both Bride & Bridegroom
INTERCASTE MARRIAGE INCENTIVE AWARD (SC Development)

The links for Application Registration/Print/status/Edit Application for Intercaste Marriage Incentive Award are available beside

GOVERNMENT ORDERS :
GO.Ms.No.12 (Guidelines)
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA :
  1. 1) One among the beneficiary, bride or bridegroom must belong to SC Community.
  2. 2) Marriages that happened after 30th October 2019 are only eligible for Inter Caste Marriage Incentive Award of 2.5 Lakhs (Others are eligible for 50,000 Rs only and they have to apply at respective District Office.)
DOCUMENTS & DETAILS REQUIRED :
  1. 1) Aadhar of both Bride and Bridegroom
  2. 2) Joint Bank Account of Bride and Bridegroom
  3. 3) Bride & Bridegroom Caste & Income Certificates
  4. 4) Marriage Registration Certificate
  5. 5) Must have atleast one Witness UID(Aadhar)
  6. 6) Permanent & Present Address details of both Bride & Bridegroom
SCHEME FLOW :
  1. 1) Intercaste Marriage couples must register their application in online website - telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in
  2. 2) The Inter caste marriage Incentive award registered applications will be enabled in DD/DSWD logins of Native District of whosoever (bride or bridegroom who belongs to SC Community) .
  3. 3) The concerned DD/DSWO will process the social status through ASWO.
  4. 4) The District welfare officer prepares the bill for verified applications and submits to Treasury.
  5. 5) The treasury passess the bill & then credit the amount in DD's Intercaste Marriage Incentive Award Account.
  6. 6) The District officer will credit the amount in Bank in the form of Fixed deposit for lock period of 3 years in the name of Inter Caste Marriage Couple.
