Fee Structure Details :

The Fee Structure under the ePASS scholarship scheme consists of two main components: Reimbursement of Tuition Fee (RTF) and Maintenance Charges (MTF).

1. Reimbursement of Tuition Fee (RTF) Ranges :

Course Group Fee Range (In Rupees)
Group-I (Professional Courses: B.Tech, Medicine, MBA, MCA) Rs. 23,000/- to Rs. 6,90,000/- per annum
Group-II (Post Graduate Courses) Rs. 3,610/- to Rs. 92,000/- per annum
Group-III (Degree Academic Courses) Rs. 1,500/- to Rs. 20,000/- per annum
Group-IV (10+2 Intermediate & ITI Courses) Rs. 850/- to Rs. 16,400/- per annum
