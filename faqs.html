Frequently Asked Questions & Student Instructions :

›

Q1. How do I apply for Post-Matric Scholarship?

Students should register online on the ePASS portal, enter mandatory details (SSC Hall Ticket, Aadhaar, Caste & Income Certificate numbers), upload documents, and submit the printed application to their respective college management.

›

Q2. Is Aadhaar Seeding with Bank Account mandatory?

Yes. As per Government guidelines, Aadhaar seeding with Nationalized Bank / India Post Payment Bank account (NPCI Seeding) is compulsory for Direct Beneficiary Transfer (DBT) of RTF and MTF amounts.

›

Q3. What should I do if my application status shows pending?

Verify whether your College Management has uploaded your biometric verification and submitted the Welfare Department booklet. You can check status online via 'Know Your Application No.'.

›

Q4. How do I submit an online grievance?

BC and EBC students can register grievances online through the Grievances Redressal portal link or contact technical support at 040-23120311 on working days.
