Telangana Welfare Schemes and Policies, in this post gives complete information about the newly formed telangana state welfare schemes. Telangana government pathakalu introduced by telangana state like epass, health cards, telangana pensions, fee reimbursement, kalyana laxmi, shaadi mubarak and many more schemes for BC,SC and ST people. In this post we discuss on the different development telangana welfare schemes and policies that are introduced by the telangana state.

›

Corporate Admissions Scheme:

Telangana Govt Corporate College Admissions for SC, ST, BC & EBC students. Check Allotment Phase - 4 status and application details.

›

Kalyana Lakshmi Pathakam:

Government decided to sanction a one-time financial assistance of Rs 1,00,116/- at the time of marriage to each SC/ST/BC/EBC girl Under Kalyana Lakshmi Pathakam.

›

Shaadi Mubaarak:

Government introduced Shaadi Mubaarak scheme for minority community unmarried girls. Rs1,00,116/- will be assisted for minority girls.

Ambedkar overseas vidya nidhi:
